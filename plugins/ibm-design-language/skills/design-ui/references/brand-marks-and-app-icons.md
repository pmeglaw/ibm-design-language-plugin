# Brand marks and app identity

Use when choosing a corporate mark, app identity, or explanatory symbol. **Selection and review advice here is local craft guidance.** Official IBM asset restrictions apply to IBM assets; using Carbon does not make another product IBM-branded. Preserve [source authority](source-map.json) and the approved [product brand](color-and-brand.md).

## Select the identity level — local heuristic

| Need | Choose | Keep distinct |
|---|---|---|
| Identify the communication's owning brand | Its approved logo or wordmark | A UI icon or app icon is not a replacement |
| Identify a product/service in a catalog or launcher | Its approved app icon and a readable product name | Product identity is different from an action such as Edit |
| Perform an action or communicate UI status | Appropriate [UI icon](icons-and-pictograms.md), with the required name/label | Do not recolor an app icon to turn it into a control glyph |
| Explain a concept or workflow | Pictogram or [illustration](imagery-and-illustration.md) | Explanatory art does not confer brand ownership |

If no approved identity asset exists, keep the identity textual while preparing a proposed asset within the task's authority. Do not fabricate an IBM affiliation or substitute the IBM mark for another product's missing logo.

## IBM logo source contracts

Source prose reviewed 2026-10-24. These are brief source summaries, not a complete brand/legal manual or evidence of permission to use a mark. Use approved artwork and the exact source guidance for production assets.

- [8-Bar](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ibm-logos/8-bar/): positive and reversed versions have different optical construction; use the appropriate supplied asset rather than simply inverting one file. The usual clear space equals the logo's height. Foreground colors belong to IBM's blue/gray core; retain proportions and unaltered bars. Place the mark against a simple, legible background. The source also routes third-party usage requests and trademark requirements; those have not been exercised here.
- [Rebus](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ibm-logos/rebus/): special applications may use the rebus, but it does not replace the 8-bar as the official sign-off. Clear space is based on the M height. Keep elements and spacing intact. The classic color version has restricted uses, requires prior brand approval and uses a black background; it is not the default contemporary palette.

The pages' palette-step separation guidance is specific to their asset system. It is not a WCAG contrast calculation or proof that text and controls placed beside the mark are accessible. Check the actual composition under [accessibility](accessibility.md).

### Placement corrections — local craft guidance

| Observation | Correction |
|---|---|
| Logo enlarged until it competes with the page task | Give identity a deliberate place in the hierarchy; protect the task title and action |
| Mark squeezed into available header space | Allocate the asset's required clear space or use a suitable approved identity treatment |
| Dark-mode CSS filter applied to every logo | Select the supplied host-appropriate artwork and inspect the actual background |
| Brand accent applied to IBM bars | Preserve the IBM mark's own approved colors; product token authority does not authorize editing third-party marks |
| Rebus, product name and app icon assembled into a new lockup | Use an approved identity asset and keep ownership and product identification legible as separate roles |

## IBM app icons: beyond UI icons

Reviewed [Design](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/app-icons/design/) and [Usage](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/app-icons/usage/) prose on 2026-10-24. The [Library](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/app-icons/library/) is the discovery route; its complete artwork was not visually audited. IBM app icons are rights restricted and identify their named offering. Retrieve an approved icon before proposing a new one.

| Official design branch, summarized | Local selection heuristic |
|---|---|
| Stroke: light linework, gradient and solid-stroke construction | Start here when identity must coexist with compact type and UI glyphs |
| Fill: geometric circle/square construction and larger color fields | Use when identity needs more visual weight; inspect recognition beside peer offerings |
| IBM Plex: type used as a product/service identifier on the icon grid | Consider only when the identity concept is typographic and the approved asset system supports it |

The Usage page distinguishes productive sizes (16, 20, 24, 32px) from expressive sizes (48px and above), with accepted scaling geometry. It supplies light/dark and monochromatic variants, host-dependent clearance and native-platform container guidance. Check the relevant asset/usage table instead of treating a 32px master as a mandatory CSS display size. Actual target size and container requirements also depend on the destination platform.

For IBM contexts, app icons are not logos: avoid icon/name lockups, product-header identity and communication sign-offs using app icons. Product/marketplace identification is a different context. Do not extend these IBM ownership rules into a ban on a separately approved brand's wordmark or icon.

### App identity review — local craft guidance

Compare a proposed asset with peers at its smallest intended desktop display size and on each actual host. Inspect silhouette, optical weight, recognition, theme variant and clearance. A detailed illustration shrunk to launcher size often loses its distinguishing idea; simplify the proposal or select an existing appropriate asset. Keep the readable name available when icon recognition is uncertain. For a clickable launcher item, verify its control semantics and accessible name separately from the artwork.

Limits: no new marks or app icons were authored; no exhaustive library, source design-file, native platform export, trademark approval, runtime or assistive-technology validation was performed. Asset availability and the permission to modify or redistribute it must be established for the actual task.
